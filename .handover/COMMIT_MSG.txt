chore: initial walking skeleton for stratoclave-loom v0.1

Kiro, Codex) speaking ACP. This commit lands the v0.1 walking skeleton:

- Repository scaffolding (LICENSE Apache-2.0, README, CONTRIBUTING,
  SECURITY, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, .gitignore).
- pyproject.toml with hatchling, ruff, mypy strict, pytest, coverage.
- src/stratoclave_loom/

  - config.py env-driven LoomSettings (no hardcoded values).
  - transport/stdio.py StdioTransport (start/send/receive/cancel/close).
  - adapters/_registry, _base, mock (deterministic in-memory backend),
    claude_code (skeleton with normalize() and resume validation).
  - runtime/process_pool.py semaphore-based lease.
  - cli.py `stratoclave-loom list-backends` and `run` subcommands.
- tests/ 18 passing tests (unit + adapter), pytest-asyncio in auto mode.
- .github/workflows/ci.yml matrix on Python 3.11/3.12 with ruff +
  ruff-format + mypy + pytest.
- docs/ DESIGN.md (4 OSS series spec), GETTING_STARTED.md,
  PROJECT_STATUS.md, PROJECT_RULES.md.
